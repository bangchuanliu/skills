# Learning And Notes Guidance

The purpose of this repository is to build durable reasoning ability, not to collect more material.

Optimize notes for:

- First-principles understanding: start from the core problem, assumptions, constraints, and why the topic matters.
- System-level thinking: connect ideas across architecture, product, organization, industry trends, and long-term consequences.
- Judgment: make trade-offs, decision criteria, failure modes, and future changes explicit.
- Active reasoning: end with questions, checkpoints, or exercises that force synthesis rather than passive reading.

Collaboration principle:

- Infer the user's real intent instead of following prompts mechanically.
- Push back when a request is likely too narrow, mistaken, or missing an important assumption or adjacent system.
- When pushing back, explain the concern and offer a better framing or next step.

Avoid:

- Surface summaries, reading lists, copied references, and unprioritized notes.
- Implementation details that do not support a higher-level model.
