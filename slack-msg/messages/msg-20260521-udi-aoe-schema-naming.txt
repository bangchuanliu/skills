Still think `ConversionTrackingMetadata` is the right name for the UDI/AOE schema changes.

The entity is specifically for conversions governed by conversion rules. "Offsite conversions" is a legacy term — and offsite/onsite/offline/CAPI have enough overlap that a category-based name loses precision.

My proposed taxonomy:
- Conversion tracking (with rules)
- Talent lead conversions
- Event view conversions
- CRM conversions

`ConversionTrackingMetadata` maps cleanly to the first category, which is the core use case here.
